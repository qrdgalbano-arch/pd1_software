# Camera setup protocol (use for every test and demo)

1. Stable stand at mat height, 2 to 3 m from the mat, on the side of the exercising leg (side view).
2. Whole body from hip to foot in view, with space around it.
3. Bright, even light from the front or above. No bright window behind the patient.
4. Fitted, plain clothing, foot visible, plain background.
5. Do not move the camera or the mat after calibration starts.
6. Confirm hip, knee and ankle are tracked in the live preview before starting.

Record camera model, resolution, distance, lighting and clothing for every validation trial.
